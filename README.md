# MaddsPet

![Version](https://shields.io)
![License](https://shields.io)

MaddsPet v1.0.0 is your interactive, highly customizable little desktop companion for Windows. Keep a small friend on your screen while you work, study, or play, with smart features that keep it non-intrusive.

---

## Features

- **Custom Pet Image Selection:** Choose and set your own favorite images to act as your desktop companion.
- **Custom Overlay Effect Color:** Personalize the look with customizable color overlays.
- **Automatic Position Saving:** The widget remembers exactly where you left it on your screen.
- **Run on Startup:** Option to automatically start MaddsPet when Windows boots up.
- **Smart Fullscreen Hiding:** Automatically hides itself when you launch fullscreen applications or games to stay out of your way.
- **Resizable and Draggable:** Freely move the widget anywhere on your desktop and scale it to your preferred size.
- **MaddsPet HUB:** Quick and easy access to settings and features via a simple right-click context menu.

---

## Installation and Setup

1. Download the latest release from the Releases page.
2. Extract the files to your preferred location on your Windows PC.
3. Run MaddsPet.exe to launch your new desktop companion.

---

## How to Use

- **Move:** Click and drag the pet anywhere on your screen.
- **Resize:** Hover near the edges or use the settings to scale your companion.
- **Settings:** Right-click on the pet to open the MaddsPet HUB to change images, adjust colors, or toggle startup options.

---

## System Requirements

- **OS:** Windows 10 / Windows 11
- Some love to your pet!
